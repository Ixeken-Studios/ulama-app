Ulama is a modern, privacy-focused football companion for Android. Follow international leagues, domestic championships, live scores, standings and tournament brackets in real time.

What's New in Version 2.0:
- Beyond the World Cup: Full support for top international leagues, club competitions, standings tables, and fixtures.
- Aesthetic Reimagination: Redesigned with Material 3 Expressive, connected lists, dynamic color schemes, and fluid motion.
- Live Notifications & Now Bar: On-device real-time match tracking and lock screen/Now Bar updates without external servers.

Key Features:
- Match Center: Live scoreboard with real-time indicators, head-to-head records, match statistics, tactical formations, and timeline.
- Comprehensive Competitions: Browse matches and tables from premier club leagues and global tournaments with radial knockout brackets.
- Favorite Teams & My Leagues: Pin your favorite clubs and national teams to prioritize them across fixtures and receive pre-match alerts.
- Match Reminders: Configurable pre-match notifications (5 to 60 minutes) and exact kickoff alarms.
- Privacy First: 100% ad-free, zero tracking, no user account required, and all preferences stored locally on your device.

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3 Expressive, and Retrofit.
